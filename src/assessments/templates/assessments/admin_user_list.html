The row shows what each control read, not its verdict. PAN-AUTH-019 reports an account unless both halves hold: authentication reaches an external identity service — RADIUS, TACACS+, LDAP, Kerberos, SAML or Cloud — and no credential is stored on the device. A bound profile is not evidence of centralization: a profile whose type is local database or none authenticates against the firewall itself, and the Authentication column says so. A stored password on a bound account is unreachable while the binding holds, and becomes live the moment somebody removes it. “Client cert only” clears neither half. The Superusers column is the appliance’s superuser total and is blank on an account that holds another role. There is no MFA column: an administrator’s second factor is enforced by the RADIUS, SAML or cloud service behind the profile, and the firewall’s configuration cannot show it.
